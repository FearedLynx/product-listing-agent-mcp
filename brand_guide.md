# Brand voice guide for product listings

These rules are the agent's instructions. The checker in `checker.py` tests the
measurable ones automatically.

## Voice
- Warm, calm and practical. Write for a parent who is short on time.
- Lead with what the product does for the child or the parent, then the details.
- Plain words. No hype, no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- British English spelling (colour, organise).

## Structure
- **Title:** brand + product name + one key detail. At most 70 characters.
- **Description:** 50 to 110 words, one paragraph.
- **Bullets:** 3 to 5 bullets, each at most 90 characters.

## Facts
- Mention every material and the age range.
- Use only facts from the product data. Never invent sizes, certifications or benefits.
- Name a certification only if the product data lists it.

## Never write
- Safety or medical promises: "100% safe", "completely safe", "prevents", "cures",
  "hypoallergenic" (unless certified), "guaranteed".
- Superlatives without proof: "the best", "perfect", "number one".
- Long dashes (— or –). Use a full stop, comma or colon instead.
